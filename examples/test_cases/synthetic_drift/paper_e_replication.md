## Abstract

We performed a preregistered replication of the exploratory NSX-4 study using 120 fictional pouch cells, with 60 coated and 60 controls. At cycle 100, coated cells retained a mean of 83.1% capacity and controls retained 82.4%, an absolute difference of +0.7 percentage points. The confidence interval was compatible with a small benefit or small harm. We found no evidence for a large improvement in capacity retention and did not test a 20-30% increase in total battery lifetime.

## Conclusion
