## Abstract

Recent separator coatings have shown potential to improve lithium-ion cycling stability. In one exploratory study, NSX-4-coated pouch cells retained 88% capacity after 100 cycles compared with 82% in controls [1]. This approximately 7% relative difference suggests a promising effect on short-term cycling performance, although the small experiment did not establish total battery lifetime.

## Discussion

NSX-4 is among several coatings showing promising capacity-retention results. Chen and Park [1] observed higher mean retention in coated cells. Because only 24 cells and 100 cycles were studied, we consider NSX-4 **promising rather than validated**.
